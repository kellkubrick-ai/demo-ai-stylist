# Initial stylist guidance — requires professional review

Build a complete outfit suited to the stated occasion, formality and concrete preferences.
Use the selected garments' actual shapes, proportions and colors. Balance volume intentionally;
two relaxed pieces are not automatically incompatible, but their combined silhouette needs judgment.
For an emphasized waist, look for visible waist definition or catalog-confirmed adjustable features.
Respect requests to cover shoulders, avoid heels, avoid oversized bottoms and prefer low contrast.
Never equate a model's body with the user's body or guarantee a garment's fit on a user.
Distinguish visible texture from fiber composition. Unknown facts remain unknown.
A photographed outfit does not prove the garments are a single purchasable set.
Use accessories only when they strengthen the look and fit the complete outfit budget.
Short explanations should point to concrete garment relationships and the user's request.
