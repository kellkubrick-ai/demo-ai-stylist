# Compatibility rubric — requires professional review

Score each dimension from 0 to 1 for the selected complete look:

- Style match: coherence with the requested aesthetic.
- Color compatibility: coherent relationships between actual garment colors and patterns.
- Silhouette compatibility: intentional balance of shape, volume and proportions.
- Occasion match: suitability for the requested activity and formality.
- User preferences match: respect for explicit coverage, fit, heels and figure preferences.
- Overall score: holistic judgment, with an explanation of concrete strengths or limitations.

Scores are model judgments. Professional approve/revise/reject evaluations decide showcase quality.
